# Email Style

## When to use
When drafting a marketing or enrollment email for The Gen Academy.

## Structure
1. **Subject** (< 50 chars): specific and curiosity-driven. No spam words, no ALL CAPS, no "!!!".
2. **Preview / first line**: reinforce the subject; don't repeat it verbatim.
3. **Body** (80–150 words): one idea, scannable. Lead with the outcome for the reader.
4. **CTA** (exactly one): a single button-style line, e.g. "Apply for the next cohort →".
5. **Sign-off**: plain and human, e.g. "— The Gen Academy team".

## Voice
Direct, warm, outcome-focused. Talk benefits ("from AI-curious to production-ready"),
not features. "Ship real work. Land real opportunities."

## Rules
- One clear CTA. Avoid multiple competing links.
- No spam triggers: "free", "guarantee", "act now", "100%", "!!!".
- Short sentences. Write for a phone screen.

# The Gen Academy: Mastering Agentic AI Certification — Enrollment Campaign Brief

- **Audience insight:** Working software engineers are interested in AI, but not blindly optimistic: Stack Overflow’s 2024 survey found 76% of developers were using or planning to use AI tools, while only 43% felt good about AI-tool accuracy and 31% were skeptical. Speak to the engineer who has tried AI tools and now wants judgment, architecture, and production habits—not another prompt list. Source: https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024
- **Market context:** AI skill demand is accelerating: WEF’s Future of Jobs Report 2025 ranks AI and big data as the fastest-growing skills, while technology roles such as AI/ML specialists and software/application developers are among the fastest-growing jobs. Frame the program as a practical bridge from curiosity to credible AI execution. Source: https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest
- **Messaging opportunities:** Lead with speed + substance: “from AI-curious to production-ready in 6 weeks.” Contrast dabbling with shipping: hands-on projects, capstone, systems thinking, portfolio building, and a bonus 7th career-development week. Use direct lines like “Stop experimenting on the sidelines. Build agentic AI systems you can actually explain, evaluate, and show.”
- **Objections to handle:** “I’m too busy” → 6-week intensive with a clear path; “I know coding, not AI” → built for AI-curious professionals through experienced engineers; “AI courses are too theoretical” → theory plus hands-on projects and capstone; “Will this help my career?” → career coaching covers job-market navigation and portfolio building, without claiming placement, salary, or promotion guarantees.
- **Proof points available from brand grounding:** The Gen Academy democratizes world-class AI education without gatekeeping; 50,000+ learners reached and 2,500+ professionals trained/upskilled; curriculum distilled from 7 years building AI systems at major tech companies; guest instructors from Google, Microsoft, Adobe, and Fireworks AI; founder Aishwarya has 500k+ LinkedIn followers and advanced degrees.
- **LinkedIn post recommendation:** Hook with the identity shift: “AI-curious engineers don’t need more hype. They need reps shipping real systems.” Body: cite the developer trust gap, name the 6-week intensive + bonus career week, list hands-on projects/capstone/guest instructors, and close with “Ship real work. Land real opportunities.” Avoid cohort dates, deadlines, scarcity, pricing, or guaranteed outcomes.
- **Marketing email recommendation:** Subject: “Go from AI-curious to production-ready in 6 weeks.” Structure: 1) acknowledge the gap between using AI tools and building reliable AI systems; 2) introduce Mastering Agentic AI Certification; 3) bullet the concrete experience—projects, capstone, production-ready curriculum, guest instructors, portfolio/career coaching; 4) CTA to explore/apply/register using only verified enrollment mechanics.
